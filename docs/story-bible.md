# Story Bible — Part 1

> **Status: DRAFT, waiting for Frank's approval.** Nothing in this file gets built until it's approved.
> Working title ideas are at the bottom.

## Premise (one paragraph)

Wren Ashby, a young apprentice mapmaker in a sleepy farming village, inherits a locket from
his eccentric aunt on the night she disappears. Inside is the **Nightglass**, a splinter of
black crystal that holds the will of the **Unlit King**, a sorcerer-tyrant who was broken an age
ago and is now gathering himself again in the east. Seven masked hunters, the **Veilborn**, come
for it. Wren flees with his two closest friends and joins a company of strangers bound for the
one place the stone can be unmade. Part 1 follows the company from its forming to its breaking.

## Tone

- Warm and pastoral at the start, then slowly colder and more desperate. Small humour carries the
  party through dark places.
- Earnest, not grimdark. Characters are kind to each other, and the cost of the journey comes
  through loss, not cruelty.
- GBA-style dialogue: short lines and a lot of character in few words. No walls of text.

## Core themes

1. **Small people, heavy burdens.** The person with the least power holds the most dangerous thing.
2. **The pull of power.** The Nightglass whispers to everyone differently, offering each person what they want most.
3. **Fellowship is chosen.** People who should distrust each other choose not to.

## The world

**The Wending Lands** is a long continent between the Western Sea and the dead plains of **Duskreach**
in the east.

| Region | Feel | Acts |
|--------|------|------|
| **The Downmeadows** | Rolling farmland, hedgerows, orchards, a lazy river | 1 |
| **The Greenway & the Wildlands** | Old road through scrub, marsh, ruined watchtowers, troll-haunted woods | 2 |
| **Velmara** | Sanctuary city of the Thessari in a hidden river valley: waterfalls, white stone, lamplight | 2 |
| **The Frostcrown Mountains** | Snowbound peaks with the old pass over them | 3 |
| **Orvanthal, the Hollow Halls** | Vast ruined underground kingdom of the Korrim | 3 |
| **Aurwyn, the Gilded Wood** | Hidden forest of golden-leaved trees, an older Thessari realm | 4 |
| **The Veyra River** | Broad river running south past ancient statues to the falls | 4 |
| **Duskreach / the Ashen Throne** | The enemy's land. Seen only in cutaways in Part 1 | — |

### Peoples (all original)

- **Folk of the Downmeadows:** ordinary humans. Farmers, millers, beekeepers. They distrust anything beyond the river.
- **The Thessari:** a long-lived, starlit people who remember the last war. Graceful, aloof, and fading from the world.
- **The Korrim:** stout mountain folk, master delvers and smiths. They lost their great kingdom, Orvanthal, three generations ago and do not say why.
- **Men of Halvard:** a proud fortress-city on the eastern front that has held back Duskreach alone for a century.
- **The Iron Brood:** brutal soldiers bred by a traitor archmage. Not a "race" in the old sense; they are made, not born.

### Lore: the elements and the spirits

The world has six elements: **Earth, Fire, Water, Wind, Light, Shadow**. The world's living soul is made
of countless **elemental spirits**.

An age ago the Unlit King, **Kael Vorhast**, tried to swallow the spirits into himself to become a god.
The other peoples broke him and shattered his crown. When he fell, the spirits he had swallowed burst
free and scattered.

Most of the freed spirits faded back into the world. The strongest ones were **caught by powerful
creatures** and still sleep inside them. Now the Nightglass, the last surviving shard of the crown, is
waking. Its pull is driving those creatures mad and turning them into monsters.

> **Gameplay tie-in:** this is why spirits come **only from bosses**. Each boss is a creature twisted
> by the spirit trapped inside it. Defeating the boss frees the spirit, and the spirit chooses to follow Wren.

### The enemy

- **The Unlit King (Kael Vorhast):** never seen in full. He appears as a lightless crown above an empty throne. He speaks through the Nightglass.
- **The Veilborn:** seven hunters in porcelain masks. Each mask was once a hero who reached for the Nightglass's power. They are recurring bosses, and each takes on an element.
  - The **Crowned Mask** is their leader.
- **Archmage Veyron:** head of the mage-order of the White Spire. He has secretly gone over to the Unlit King and breeds the Iron Brood. In Part 1 he is revealed at the council but never fought; he is a Part 2 villain.
- **The Hushed:** the Ashen Throne's network of spies and informants.

### The goal

The Nightglass can only be unmade where it was forged: **the Dusk Furnace** in the heart of Duskreach.
Carrying it there is the goal of the whole saga. Part 1 ends long before they arrive.

## Act 1: Homeland (~2h)

**Setting:** the Downmeadows. **Party at the end:** Wren, Tobin, Jun (+ Isolde if recruited).

1. **Lantern Night (prologue, Brindlewick).** It is the village's harvest festival. Aunt **Hesper Ashby**, a retired adventurer who never talks about her past, gives a strange toast and then **vanishes**.
   - She leaves Wren her cottage, a letter, and an iron locket.
   - Tutorial: walking, talking, shops, and a first battle against orchard pests, fought alongside the travelling sage **Maelis the Lamplighter** (temporary guest).
2. **The stone wakes.** Maelis recognises the Nightglass and is shaken. She tells Wren to leave quietly for the frontier town of **Harrow's Rest** and promises to meet him at the Lantern Inn. Then she rides off to consult the White Spire. *(Maelis leaves the party.)*
3. **Leaving home.** Wren's best friend **Tobin** refuses to let him go alone. Wren's cousin **Jun** catches them sneaking out and invites herself along.
4. **Thornhedge Lane.** The first route. The Nightglass's waking has driven a great boar mad. **Boss: Old Bristleback (Earth).** Its spirit, *Burrow*, is the player's first spirit, with a big acquisition scene and a spirit tutorial.
5. **A rider on the road.** A Veilborn passes. This is a hide-and-sneak scene; the player is told to avoid the hunter, and nothing fails if they're seen.
6. **(Optional) The Sedgewood.** In a sickened marsh forest lives **Isolde Fenwick**, a hedge-witch the village drove out years ago. **Optional boss: the Bog Matron (Water)** → Isolde joins.
7. **The Old Cairns.** Fog leads the party into ancient burial mounds. A dead king's wight takes them.
   - Wren's earth sense awakens: **field ability Push learned**.
   - **Boss: the Cairn Warden (Shadow).** The party finds old cairn-steel blades in the tomb.
8. **Ferrygate.** The Veilborn close in at night. There is a chase to the river ferry, and the hunter blocks the dock. **Boss: the Mask of Gales (Wind).** The ferry pushes off and the hunter can't follow across water. Act 1 ends as the lights of home disappear behind them.

**Cutaway:** an empty throne. A lightless crown turns its "gaze" west.

## Act 2: The Road & the Haven (~2.5h)

**Setting:** the Greenway, the Wildlands, Velmara. **Party at the end:** the company is formed.

1. **Harrow's Rest.** A rough frontier town. Maelis never arrives.
   - In the Lantern Inn's common room, the Nightglass flares and reveals Wren to the town's Hushed spies.
   - A hooded ranger called "**the Hawk**" (**Corvin Hale**) pulls them out of the window and onto the back roads. **Corvin joins.**
   - **(Optional)** **Vesk**, a jittery informant, begs for help: they deserted the Hushed and the spymaster wants them dead. **Optional boss: Spymaster Quell (Shadow)** → Vesk joins.
2. **The Fenlow Marshes.** A miserable bog route with insect swarms. Camping, banter, and Corvin's backstory.
3. **Watchfire Hill.** They camp in a ruined beacon tower. Five Veilborn attack at night. **Boss: the Mask of Embers (Fire).**
   - The mask stabs Wren with a shard-blade before it is driven off. Wren is **wounded**: the cold spreads toward his heart.
   - *(Wren is locked out of the party; Tobin leads, and Wren is carried on a litter.)*
4. **Trollgate Woods.** A shortcut through a wood of hulking stone ogres. **Boss: the Stone Ogres (Earth, twin fight).** They turn to stone at dawn: survive or win.
5. **The Brightwash Ford.** A Thessari envoy, **Sorrel**, meets them and races them to the river ford with the Veilborn behind. **Boss: the Mask of Tides (Water)** at the ford.
   - Mid-fight, the Thessari call up the river. The flood (a telegraphed attack that hits both sides) washes the hunters away.
6. **Velmara.** The sanctuary city.
   - Wren wakes up healed. *(Wren is unlocked.)*
   - Maelis is here; she escaped imprisonment in the White Spire.
   - **The Council of Lamps:**
     - Maelis reveals Archmage Veyron's betrayal.
     - **Bruna Ironvein** of the Korrim and **Lord Cassius Varr** of Halvard argue over the Nightglass. Cassius wants to use it to defend his city. Everyone else refuses.
     - Wren volunteers to carry it. The company forms: Wren, Tobin, Jun, Corvin, Maelis, Bruna, Cassius. **Maelis, Bruna, and Cassius join.**
   - The Thessari gift a charm that grants **field ability Gust**.
   - **(Optional quest start)** A Thessari matriarch asks the company to find her daughter **Ithra**, who went into the Deep after a lost expedition.

## Act 3: The Mountain & the Deep (~3h)

**Setting:** the Frostcrown and Orvanthal. **Party at the end:** the company, minus Maelis (+ Ithra if recruited).

1. **The Ashvale Ruins.** The company travels south along the mountains. Flocks of carrion kites spy for the enemy, and an optional detour avoids them.
2. **Frostcrown Pass.** They try to cross the mountains. A blizzard rises as if the mountain itself rejects them. **Boss: Skarn, the Mountain's Wrath (Water/ice).** Even after the win, the pass is buried. They turn back.
3. **The Gate of Orvanthal.** Bruna leads them to her people's lost door. A riddle-door puzzle is **required and simple**. Something rises from the black lake. **Boss: the Mere Lurker (Shadow).** The door slams behind them, and the only way is through.
4. **Orvanthal, the Hollow Halls.** A multi-floor dungeon with **camps** between floors.
   - The party finds **the Delver's Chain**, a Korrim relic that grants **field ability Grasp**.
   - **(Optional) The Silent Halls:** a side wing where **Ithra Sael** is trapped. She is guarding the bodies of her lost expedition. **Optional boss: the Hollow Seraph (Light)**, a fallen star-guardian. → Ithra joins.
5. **The Hall of Records.** Bruna finds the tomb of her lost king and his journal ("They woke it. We could not put it back to sleep."). War drums sound. **Boss: Gorrak Blacktusk & the Cave Brute (Earth).**
6. **The Chasm Bridge.** The ancient terror wakes. **Boss: Uldraeth, the Furnace Below (Fire, multi-phase).**
   - When its last phase begins, Maelis steps onto the bridge alone, breaks it, and falls into the dark with the beast. *(Maelis is removed from the roster for Part 1. Her equipment returns to the bag.)*
   - **Uldraeth drops 2 spirits:** *Bellows* (Fire), and **Vigil** (Light), born from Maelis's last light.
7. **Daylight.** The company comes out onto a hillside. A grief scene: no music, then each character reacts.

## Act 4: The Golden Wood & the River (~2.5h)

**Setting:** Aurwyn and the Veyra River. **Party at the end:** broken (see Ending).

1. **Aurwyn, the Gilded Wood.** The company rests among the Thessari of the wood, ruled by **Warden Amaranth**. Shops, an inn, and quiet character scenes; the safest place in the game.
   - **The Heartmere:** a still pool that shows visions. Wren sees the Downmeadows burning, then a version of himself wearing a lightless crown. Amaranth refuses the Nightglass when Wren offers it.
   - **(Optional) Trial of the Heartmere:** if Ithra is in the party, Amaranth offers a trial. **Optional boss: the Mirror Warden (Light).**
   - Parting gifts: boats, travel cloaks (**encounter-reducing equipment**), and rope.
2. **The Veyra River.** Boat travel between landing points. Iron Brood raiders shadow them along the banks.
   - Cassius is quieter each day. The Nightglass is whispering to him.
3. **The Teeth.** At a portage around the rapids, an Iron Brood war-band springs an ambush. **Boss: Hagra the Pyre-Witch (Fire).**
4. **The Twin Wardens.** The boats pass two colossal statues of ancient kings. This is a non-interactive scenic moment that hints at Corvin's heritage.
5. **Hollow Hill.** The company camps to decide its route.
   - **Cassius's fall:** alone with Wren, Cassius begs, then tries to take the Nightglass. **Scripted duel vs Cassius (Fire).** No spirit drop; you can't truly win or lose it. Wren flees.
   - **The breaking:** the Iron Brood attack in force, led by the warlord **Skarrak**. The party fights a series of wave battles.
   - Drawn by the stone, the **Crowned Mask** descends. **Final boss: the Crowned Mask (Wind, drops 2).**
   - **The aftermath:**
     - Cassius returns, ashamed, and fights to defend Jun, but both are overwhelmed. Skarrak's brood carry Jun off. Cassius is dragged away, alive.
     - Wren understands that the stone will tear any company apart. He takes a boat to cross the river alone. Tobin wades in after him and won't be left behind.
     - Corvin, Bruna (and Ithra, Isolde, and Vesk if recruited) choose to hunt for Jun.

## Ending: the cliffhanger

1. On the far shore, Wren and Tobin look east toward Duskreach.
2. **Cutaway: the Ashen Throne.** The Crowned Mask places an **eighth porcelain mask** before a chained Cassius.
   *"Seven were never enough."*
3. **Final beat:** that night, the Nightglass whispers Wren's name for the first time. It speaks in **Aunt Hesper's voice**.
4. **TO BE CONTINUED.**
