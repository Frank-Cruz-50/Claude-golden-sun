## 1. Wren Ashby: the Bearer

- **Element:** Earth.
- **Role:** balanced adept. He can fight or cast, with no weak stat.
- **Stats:** HP 3, PP 3, ATK 3, DEF 3, AGI 3.
- **Age:** 19. **Pronouns:** he/him.
- **Recruited:** the protagonist (story, from the start). **Locked in** except while wounded in Act 2.
- **Who:** an apprentice mapmaker in Brindlewick. He was raised by his aunt Hesper after his parents drowned in a river flood. He has drawn maps of places he never expected to visit.
- **Personality:** careful, observant, quietly funny. Hates being the centre of attention.
- **Arc:** wants a small, safe life → learns the burden can't be handed off → *chooses* to carry it alone at the end of Part 1, and finds out he can't really be alone (Tobin).
- **Sample abilities:** Stonecall (Earth damage), Mend Soil (light heal), Bulwark (DEF up on self).
- **Nightglass pull:** offers him a world where nothing ever changes and nobody he loves leaves.

## 2. Tobin Marsh: the Loyal Friend

- **Element:** Water.
- **Role:** healer and support.
- **Stats:** HP 3, PP 4, ATK 2, DEF 3, AGI 2.
- **Age:** 20. **Pronouns:** he/him.
- **Recruited:** Act 1, Brindlewick (story).
- **Who:** the son of Hesper's gardener. A cook, an herbalist, and Wren's oldest friend. He brings a frying pan everywhere and uses it as a weapon.
- **Personality:** warm, stubborn, frightened of almost everything, brave anyway.
- **Arc:** sees himself as a sidekick → leads the party while Wren is wounded (Act 2) → refuses to be left behind at the end.
- **Sample abilities:** Spring Tonic (heal one), Cleanse (cure status), Rain Ward (party heal-over-time).

## 3. Juniper "Jun" Pell: the Troublemaker

- **Element:** Fire.
- **Role:** fast striker. Multi-hit and critical-hit focused.
- **Stats:** HP 2, PP 2, ATK 4, DEF 2, AGI 5.
- **Age:** 17. **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Recruited:** Act 1, Brindlewick (story).
- **Who:** Wren's younger cousin. A sling-and-knife scrapper who treats the whole quest like a lark at first.
- **Personality:** loud, reckless, funny. Fiercely loyal and secretly scared of being useless.
- **Arc:** treats danger as a game → is shaken by Maelis's fall → grows up fast in Act 4 → is captured at the ending (Part 2 hook).
- **Sample abilities:** Flurry (3 random hits), Firecracker (Fire damage, may blind), Dash Strike (acts first).

## 4. Isolde Fenwick: the Hedge-Witch *(optional)*

- **Element:** Water.
- **Role:** hexer. Status effects, debuffs, water magic.
- **Stats:** HP 2, PP 5, ATK 1, DEF 2, AGI 3.
- **Age:** 40s. **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Recruited:** Act 1, the Sedgewood. **Side quest: "The Sickened Mire."** Defeat the Bog Matron, which has been poisoning the marsh.
- **Who:** Brindlewick drove her out as a "witch" fifteen years ago after a fever season she was blamed for. She has lived alone in the marsh ever since.
- **Personality:** dry, sharp-tongued, privately kind. Talks to frogs.
- **Arc:** bitterness → she protects the very village kids who once threw stones at her (Jun) → a reconciliation scene if the party returns to Brindlewick (Part 2).
- **Sample abilities:** Mire Hex (lower AGI), Leech Draught (poison), Stillwater (sleep).

## 5. Corvin Hale: "the Hawk"

- **Element:** Wind.
- **Role:** ranger swordsman. A strong physical all-rounder.
- **Stats:** HP 4, PP 2, ATK 4, DEF 3, AGI 4.
- **Age:** 34. **Pronouns:** he/him.
- **Recruited:** Act 2, Harrow's Rest (story).
- **Who:** a wandering ranger who has watched the borderlands for years under a false name. Secretly the last heir of the drowned kingdom of **Ostreth**. (He doesn't reveal it in Part 1; the Twin Wardens only hint at it.)
- **Personality:** reserved, wry, patient. Gentle with the young ones.
- **Arc:** a man running from a crown → takes up command after Maelis falls → chooses Jun over the stone at the ending.
- **Sample abilities:** Gale Edge (Wind damage), Hawk's Eye (guaranteed crit next turn), Watchman (draws attacks).

## 6. Vesk: the Deserter *(optional)*

- **Element:** Shadow.
- **Role:** rogue. Speed, stealing, poison, evasion.
- **Stats:** HP 2, PP 3, ATK 3, DEF 2, AGI 5.
- **Age:** 26. **Pronouns:** they/them.
- **Recruited:** Act 2, Harrow's Rest. **Side quest: "A Debt in the Dark."** Get Vesk out of town alive by defeating Spymaster Quell in the undercity.
- **Who:** a former informant for the Hushed, the enemy's spy network. They sold secrets for years until they found out what the secrets were used for.
- **Personality:** nervous, wisecracking, compulsively honest about their own dishonesty.
- **Arc:** trusts no one → becomes the company's eyes → offers to sneak into Duskreach to find Jun (Part 2 hook).
- **Sample abilities:** Pilfer (steal an item), Nightshade (poison), Fade (evade the next attack).
- **Bonus:** adds optional dialogue about enemy plans throughout Acts 2–4.

## 7. Maelis the Lamplighter: the Mentor

- **Element:** Light.
- **Role:** mage. Strong offensive and support magic, and the highest PP in the roster.
- **Stats:** HP 2, PP 5, ATK 2, DEF 2, AGI 3.
- **Age:** looks 60 and is much older. **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Recruited:**
  - Act 1 prologue: a guest (locked in, then leaves).
  - Act 2: rejoins at Velmara (story).
  - Act 3: **lost** at the Chasm Bridge. Her equipment and any held spirits return to the bag.
- **Who:** a wandering sage who lights the lamps of forgotten shrines. One of the few still alive who saw the Unlit King fall. An old friend of Hesper's.
- **Personality:** grandmotherly, mischievous, terrifying when angry. Loves bad puns and lights candles that don't need lighting.
- **Arc:** guides from the shadows → sacrifices herself so the company can live. Her last light becomes the spirit **Vigil**.
- **Sample abilities:** Lantern Flare (Light damage to all), Sanctuary (party DEF and resistance up), Kindly Light (revive).
- **Design note:** she's deliberately strong. Players should feel her loss in battle, not just in the story.

## 8. Bruna Ironvein: the Shield of the Korrim

- **Element:** Earth.
- **Role:** tank and guardian.
- **Stats:** HP 5, PP 2, ATK 3, DEF 5, AGI 1.
- **Age:** 112 (young for a Korrim). **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Recruited:** Act 2, Velmara council (story).
- **Who:** a Korrim warrior sent to the council to ask for news of her people's lost kingdom, Orvanthal. Fights with a hammer and a door-sized shield.
- **Personality:** blunt, proud, loud-laughing. Distrusts the Thessari on principle.
- **Arc:** wants to reclaim her ancestors' halls → finds their tomb and the truth of their fall (Act 3) → grief turns into a reason to keep going.
  - If Ithra is recruited, their rivalry turns into the best friendship in the party.
- **Sample abilities:** Guard (protect an ally), Quake Hammer (Earth damage to all), Stand Firm (taunt + DEF up).

## 9. Lord Cassius Varr: the Proud Son

- **Element:** Fire.
- **Role:** heavy warrior. Slow, with huge single hits.
- **Stats:** HP 4, PP 2, ATK 5, DEF 4, AGI 1.
- **Age:** 30. **Pronouns:** he/him.
- **Recruited:** Act 2, Velmara council (story).
  - Act 4: **locked out** before the betrayal duel.
  - Ending: captured. He is the eighth Veilborn cliffhanger.
- **Who:** the eldest son of the Steward of Halvard, a fortress-city that has bled for a century holding back Duskreach. A brilliant, beloved soldier.
- **Personality:** charismatic, generous, protective. Cannot stand helplessness.
- **Arc:** a good man who wants to save his people → the stone feeds on that love → tries to take it → repents and defends Jun → is taken.
- **Sample abilities:** Brandstrike (Fire damage, slow), War Cry (party ATK up), Last Stand (stronger at low HP).
- **Nightglass pull:** offers him the power to save Halvard alone.

## 10. Ithra Sael: the Starborn Archer *(optional)*

- **Element:** Wind.
- **Role:** archer. Multi-target attacks and the best AGI in the game.
- **Stats:** HP 2, PP 3, ATK 4, DEF 2, AGI 5.
- **Age:** 400 (young for the Thessari). **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Recruited:** Act 3, the Silent Halls of Orvanthal. **Side quest: "The Silent Expedition."** It starts in Velmara (Act 2) and is completed by defeating the Hollow Seraph.
- **Who:** a Thessari scout who went into the Deep alone to find her brother's lost expedition. She found it, and everyone was dead.
- **Personality:** cool, precise, quietly grieving. Has a sharp, very dry sense of humour.
- **Arc:** cold grief → finds a new company to protect → friendship with Bruna.
  - Her presence unlocks the Act 4 optional trial (the Mirror Warden).
- **Sample abilities:** Volley (hits all enemies), Pinning Shot (delays enemy turn), Starsight (party accuracy up).
